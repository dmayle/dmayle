# Douglas Mayle

Founder of [3 & Done](https://3anddoneapp.com): three things that matter, emailed every morning.

I've written software for a living for twenty-five years, most of it at YouTube, Facebook and Google: the work that let YouTube take videos longer than fifteen minutes, its first live streams, Facebook's News Feed, a billion-account migration at Google Wallet, and a team that put voice controls into cars running Android.

Along the way I abandoned twenty or thirty task systems, because every one of them left the work of managing the list to me. 3 & Done is the one that does that work itself. You dump everything on your mind, typed or spoken, as messy as it comes. Each morning it emails you exactly three things that matter today, with the reason each made the cut.

Try it without signing up at [3anddoneapp.com/try](https://3anddoneapp.com/try). The press kit is at [3anddoneapp.com/press](https://3anddoneapp.com/press).
